Frost Mage on the Tartarus V2

Wesheavyn, level 29 Troll Frost Mage, with the Tartarus under the left hand and the Scimitar under the right. Everything here is checked against the Forever beta spell data, not Classic. Greyed-out spells are ones you have not learned yet.

Do this now

  1. Type /reload.
  2. Log in, stand still out of combat for five seconds. Chat says "Layout applied" and lists what moved.
  3. Type /torch map to see every key in chat.
  4. Play for an hour, then type /torch stats. It tells you what you clicked, what you fumbled and what did nothing.
  5. Do not like it? /torch undo puts the bars and binds back exactly as they were before any of this.

You never run a setup command. New ranks, new spells, a better potion, a new scroll or your first mount all go to their key by themselves.

What changed on October 10

  • Each bar is now one device. Left hand on the main bar, left hand + Shift above it, and your mouse thumb buttons as two blocks in the bottom right, shaped like the mouse. No key changed, only where the squares sit.
  • Every mage spell in Forever has a home. Combat spells on keys, buffs, conjures, teleports and portals on the click bars, and any spell the addon has never heard of is still put on a bar.
  • Your pinky has nothing to do. The Ctrl layer is gone. Its spells moved to the mouse thumb grid, with Shift (your thumb) as the only modifier.
  • Ice Lance, Cone of Cold, Fire Blast, Counterspell, Blink and Ice Block cancel your cast and fire at once. The price is real: press one while it is on cooldown and you still lose the cast you were making.
  • Rank 1 Frostbolt is on thumb + R. A 1.5 second cast for the slow, not the damage.
  • The bandage always bandages you. Health potion and bandage both stop your cast first. Each key uses the best one in your bags that you are high enough level for.
  • Decurse: hold thumb Shift and press 3 with the mouse over anyone or any party frame. Or middle-click a party, raid or player frame.
  • Mouse 4 and mouse 5 are bound to Polymorph and Counterspell, waiting for the mouse fix below.
  • Wheel click targets the next enemy, so you do not need the pinky on Tab.

Start with the top two rows. Thumb 1 Polymorph and thumb 4 Counterspell are the two that matter in a panic. Hold the pad's thumb Shift with any button for the orange spell. The mount, mana potion and grenade buttons stay empty until you own one, then fill themselves.

How you move

Your left hand stays on the pad the whole time. W A S D are on the pad itself.

  • Forward and back: pad keys 08 (W) and 13 (S), middle finger.
  • Sidestep: pad keys 12 (A) and 14 (D), ring and index finger. They strafe, they do not turn.
  • Turn with the mouse. Hold the right mouse button and you face wherever the camera points.
  • Hold both mouse buttons to run forward. That frees your whole left hand for spells.
  • Instant spells are cast on the move: Frost Nova, Ice Lance, Fire Blast, Blink. Frostbolt makes you stand still, which is why you slow things first.

The small number in each key's corner is the number printed on the pad. It is only there so you can find the key. It has nothing to do with "Action Button" numbers in the game's menus. Go by the letter.

The thumb d-pad is 8-way. Press straight up, down, left or right. A diagonal press sends two directions at once and can use the wrong item.

Five habits

  • Nova, step out, Ice Lance, Frostbolt. Q freezes them. Sidestep out of melee range, press R while you move (Ice Lance is instant and does 300% more damage to a frozen target), then start Frostbolt. Damage can break the freeze, so move first.
  • Ice Lance only on a frozen target. A slowed mob is not frozen. On a mob that is only chilled it does 30 damage and wastes the cast.
  • Fire Blast finishes. Z is instant. Use it on the run or to end a mob at a sliver of health.
  • Keep Blink for stuns and roots when you can. F breaks both.
  • Never click a combat spell. If you catch yourself clicking one, it needs a key. Tell me which and it gets one.
  • Interrupts and sheep go on the mouse. Thumb 4 and thumb 1. Your left hand never stops moving to cast them.
  • Do not mash the instant keys during a cast you want. R, Z and 2 now cancel Frostbolt the moment you press them. That is what you asked for, and it means a nervous early press throws the Frostbolt away.
  • Rank 1 Frostbolt (thumb + R) is for runners and kiting. In the current Forever beta it no longer triggers Frostbite or any other talent at 60, so it is a slow and nothing else.
  • Bandage after a Polymorph or a Nova, pad right. It always lands on you now, whatever you have targeted.

Focus in one example

Your target is the enemy you are killing. Your focus is a second enemy the game remembers for you, with its own portrait. A focus macro casts on that second enemy without dropping your target.

Thumb Shift + Tab (or Shift + wheel click) saves whoever you are targeting as your focus.

  1. Two mobs. Click the dangerous one and press Shift + wheel click. It is now your focus.
  2. Hold thumb Shift and press mouse thumb 1 (or pad 1). Polymorph lands on it.
  3. Press Tab and kill the other mob.
  4. The sheep breaks early. Shift + 1 again. It is sheeped again and you never changed target.
  5. First mob dies. Click the sheep, or press Tab, and finish it.

Polymorph only works on Beasts, Humanoids and Critters, and any damage breaks it, so keep Blizzard and Arcane Explosion away from the sheep. Counterspell works the same way: Shift + 4 interrupts your focus without dropping your target.

When Frostbolt does not work

A few mobs are immune to Frost, mostly water and ice elementals. You cannot slow or freeze them, so they will reach you. These are Forever beta tooltip numbers with no gear and no talents, read from the game data, not tested in a fight.

  1. Open with Fireball from max range. It reaches 35 yards. Frostbolt, Scorch and Arcane Missiles reach 30 and Fire Blast 20.
  2. Then Arcane Missiles. It is the stronger of the two per second. It is a channel, so every hit you take cuts it short.
  3. Getting beaten on? Scorch. Short casts lose less to being hit. It is the weakest, so it is the fallback.
  4. Fire Blast whenever it is ready.

Always use your highest rank. Against anything that can be chilled, Frostbolt stays your main spell: its slow is what keeps mobs off you, and your talents are in Frost.

What the evidence says

  • Every well-known mage found plays keyboard and mouse. None uses a keypad. Only one has a sourced Classic layout, Xaryu in 2019, and it covers about a dozen binds. Nobody's Forever mage binds are published yet.
  • They all agree on one thing: instant, reactive spells on the mouse, the cast rotation on the left hand. Nobody puts Frostbolt on a thumb grid. Your layout follows that.
  • Tartarus players: of 26 WoW users found, 17 move with the thumb pad and 9 keep movement on the keys like you. People who run a keypad together with a 12-button mouse (14 found) mostly split it your way.
  • Weak pinky: the one player found with the same problem and the same kind of mouse was told to take every modifier off the left pinky. That is why Ctrl is gone here.
  • Stop-casting on instants is universal on Counterspell and common on Blink and Ice Block. On damage instants it is rarer, because it skips the game's spell queue.
  • No measured comparison of keypad against keyboard exists. Your own stats are the only real test. Give each two weeks.

Counts come from comments under keypad videos and a vendor forum, so people who gave the pad up are under-counted. Reddit could not be read.

If something is wrong

  • A key does nothing or does the wrong thing: type /torch binds. It re-applies every bind on this page.
  • A spell is on the wrong key: /torch setup forces the layout again. Anything of yours in the way is moved to a free slot, not deleted.
  • You moved a key on purpose: it stays. The addon only fills empty slots and updates ranks after the first run.
  • Back to how it was: /torch undo.
  • Bind one square yourself: Esc, Options, Keybindings, the red Quick Keybind Mode button. Hover a square, press the key, click Okay.
  • The thumb button is not acting as Shift: tell me. That is the pad remap, not the game.
  • Slow Fall (Shift + mouse thumb 8) needed a Light Feather in Classic. If it will not cast, that is why.
  • You cannot find a buff or a scroll: there are 18 click squares. Anything past that is on Action Bar 6. Switch it on under Options, Action Bars.
  • The mouse blocks cover something, or sit in the wrong place: Esc, Edit Mode, drag them. Or /torch look rows.
