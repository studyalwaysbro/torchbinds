## 0. Two WoW: Forever facts that change the design (found while researching)

1. Rank 1 Frostbolt no longer procs anything at 60.
   - Teddys, "WoW Forever Detailed Mage PvP Guide From a Top Player", 2026-10-05,
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UHCIig2Br3A . He reads the patch note: spells cast
     at ranks far below your level "have a reduced chance to activate your class abilities
     and talents ... Using a rank one frostbolt at level 60 will result in a 0% chance to
     get a frostbite activation". He says it also applies to Winter's Chill, Fingers of
     Frost and Arcane Concentration. He still expects mages to cast rank 1 Frostbolt
     "quite a lot" for the fast slow.
   - Doomsong Forever, frost leveling guide, 2026-10-03,
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q7zfFmYH_1E : "a level 60 mage using rank one
     frostbolt gets zero chance to proc frostbite ... don't build your frost rotation
     around fishing for procs using rank one frostbolt. Downgrade can still have utility".
   - Before that patch (2026-09-22) Venruki, Xaryu and Ziqo expected the opposite:
     "Frost is just going to be all about spamming rank one frostbolts, fishing for procs"
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PG0SD-AXZI . That advice is now stale.
   - Same Teddys video: Cone of Cold slow 8s -> 6s; Improved Blizzard slow cut to 40%;
     rank 1 Blizzard no longer procs Frostbite/Fingers of Frost.
   - Consequence: rank 1 Frostbolt in Forever is a slow-only button (1.0s cast with
     Improved Frostbolt per classic sources below). It should not be the Winter's Chill
     stacker or the proc fisher.
   - Beta caveat: all of this is beta state; both creators say it may change.

2. Forever uses the modern client's keybind system.
   - Arwoht, "WoW Forever Keybind Guide For Beginners", 2026-10-04,
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=shgvNTr6PEA : shows Edit Mode, "Quick Keybind Mode"
     (hover a button, press the key; Escape clears it), and the camera settings.
   - Gotcha he demonstrates: if `Shift-C` is itself bound to an action button, a macro on
     `C` with `[mod:shift]` never sees the shift press. "The game prioritizes this keybind
     on the action bar over this modifier". An addon that writes both direct modified
     binds and `[mod]` macros on the same base key will break one of them.
   - Hunter-only detail from the same video: Ctrl-1..5 are the default pet bar binds.
   - Fairbanks macro videos (2026-09-23 / 09-25) show `[@cursor] Blizzard`,
     `[@mouseover,harm,nodead][]`, and the spell name "Remove Lesser Curse" working in the
     Forever beta: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IeebkjoO894 ,
     https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lg89AbEBOp0 .

His stated rules (same videos)
- One modifier means one thing: Shift + X is always the mouseover (Classic) or focus
  (retail) version of X.
- Spammable and chained spells go on unmodified keys that can be hit in sequence
  (Tab, 3, 1). "It shouldn't be modifiers for these simple spammable keybindings."
- Modifiers are for less frequent things (Shift+3 gem, Alt+1 Fire Ward, Shift+5).
- Ice Block lives on the mouse so it works "no matter what I'm doing with my left hand".

## 2. Conventions among strong Classic mages

### /stopcasting on instants
- Counterspell: universal. Xaryu: "I would recommend to have a stopcasting into at least
  Counterspell" (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CsiWYl_SKuM). Megaphone pastebin:
  `/stopcasting /cancelaura Ice Block /cast Counterspell`. FrostLive doc, Toxicyo
  (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11PlJJ2iRGw, Classic 2019), Fairbanks (Forever beta).
- Also seen on: Blink (Fairbanks "Emergency Blink"; briankoponen), Ice Block (Toxicyo,
  FrostLive, warcraft.wiki.gg), Cold Snap (Toxicyo, briankoponen), Fire Blast and Frost
  Nova (briankoponen, https://www.briankoponen.com/world-warcraft-classic-mage-macros/),
  Presence of Mind + Pyroblast (Fairbanks), Polymorph (briankoponen only; Megaphone
  instead uses the Polymorph button deliberately as his "stop" via /petpassive).
- Xaryu does NOT put it on everything: only Counterspell in his shown list.
- Known downsides, with sources:
  - It breaks spell queueing. "using /stopcasting breaks the Spell Queue Window"
    (Elvenbane, EU forums, 2021-04,
    https://eu.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/stopcasting-castsequence-macro-bug/268854).
    A mage testing `/stopcasting /cast Ice Barrier` mid-Frostbolt: "it stops the FB cast,
    but doesn't do the Ice Barrier, unless I keep tapping the key"; reply: "You're still
    GCD locked half way through a Frostbolt cast and iirc stopcasting bypasses the
    SpellQueueWindow so it won't queue up the 2nd spell. Try it with something that's off
    the GCD" (https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/help-with-caststopcast-macro/777987,
    2023-03). RETAIL forum, but the client is the same generation as Forever's.
    Practical reading: /stopcasting is clean on off-GCD spells (Counterspell, Ice Block,
    Cold Snap) and costs a wasted cast plus a possible dead press on GCD instants
    (Fire Blast, Nova, Cone of Cold, Ice Barrier, Ice Lance).
  - It kills a cast you wanted. Any panic press of a /stopcasting button during the last
    tenth of a Frostbolt throws the Frostbolt away. No source needed beyond the macro
    semantics, but note that this is the main risk for a panicky player.
  - Channel protection alternative: Megaphone's Ice Lance uses `[nochanneling]` instead
    of /stopcasting so it cannot clip Blizzard/Evocation.
  - An older claim that spamming a stopcasting Frostbolt macro cancels the cast on the
    server (lurkerlounge thread surfaced by search): page would not load. Not verified.

### Ice Block cancel
- Three patterns found:
  1. Cancel folded into the filler: Xaryu's Frostbolt macro and Toxicyo's
     (`/cast Frostbolt /cancelaura Ice Block`); Hansol does it on Inferno Blast.
     Pressing your nuke exits the block. Megaphone folds `/cancelaura Ice Block` into
     Counterspell, Polymorph and rank 1 Frostbolt arena macros.
  2. Same key toggles: `/cast Ice Block /cancelaura Ice Block` (Megaphone pastebin;
     warcraft.wiki.gg "Press to cast Ice Block, and press again to remove it",
     https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Useful_macros_for_mages). Danger: double-tap in a
     panic removes the block instantly.
  3. Modifier-gated cancel: FrostLive `/stopcasting /cast Ice Block /cancelaura
     [modifier:ctrl] Ice Block` -- "if I hold control and press my ice block button then
     it cancels". Safest of the three for someone who mashes.
- Warning about pattern 2 for mashers: stated in the search summary of Icy Veins /
  Warcraft Tavern guides; Icy Veins itself returned 403, so that wording is not verified.

### Rank 1 Frostbolt and rank choices
- PvP, why: "it's a one-second cast... 25 mana... get a slow on someone very
  efficiently". Top rank for damage, bottom rank "for slowing people, proccing
  frostbite" (Xaryu, TBC Classic, 2021-06-11,
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hCoQVZyjZc0). Snagz, Classic mage PvP guide,
  2019-08-18, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqyJu0KMBO4 : with Improved Frostbolt
  "rank 1 frostbolt has a 1 second cast... you can run around and kite people incredibly
  effectively".
- PvE, why: Winter's Chill stacking at low mana. "Cast Frostbolt Rank 1 to quickly stack
  Winter's Chill... sacrifice the first 5 seconds of the fight to cast 5 x Rank 1
  Frostbolts" (Warcraft Tavern Classic frost PvE guide,
  https://www.warcrafttavern.com/wow-classic/guides/pve-frost-mage-rotations-cooldowns/).
  In Forever this no longer works at 60 (section 0).
- Megaphone pastebin (TBC): "Rank 1 Polymorph and Frost Nova ALWAYS. Rank 1 Cone of Cold
  and Blizzard FREQUENTLY. Rank 1 Frostbolt for control / Arcane Concentration procs
  FREQUENTLY. Max rank Cone of Cold and Blizzard should not be used unless you are sure
  it's going to kill."
- Other downranks named: rank 1 Arcane Explosion to break stealth (Xaryu, Snagz), rank 1
  Fire Blast for totems and Spell Reflect bait (Xaryu), rank 1 Cone of Cold to kite
  (Xaryu: 210 vs 645 mana in TBC), rank 1 Frost Nova always (Xaryu: "I don't even have"
  the higher ranks on bars), rank 1 Arcane Intellect / Dampen Magic as dispel padding.
- How people bind it: two styles.
  - Separate key: Xaryu, rank 1 Frostbolt on 5.
  - Modifier on the same key: FrostLive `[nomodifier] Frostbolt(Rank 10) / [modifier:shift]
    Frostbolt(Rank 1)`; Fairbanks for Forever `/cast [mod:shift] Frostbolt(Rank 1);
    Frostbolt`. Megaphone inverts it for Cone of Cold: default rank 1, Alt = max rank.
  - For a weak pinky the separate-key style avoids holding Shift for the most-spammed
    kiting button.

### Mouseover and focus
- Classic has no focus frame, so the convention is mouseover on a modifier. Xaryu: Shift
  + key = mouseover Polymorph / Counterspell / Fire Blast; decurse macro is
  mouseover-else-self. Toxicyo, FrostLive, Fairbanks all ship
  `[@mouseover,harm,nodead][] Polymorph` style macros.
- Whether Forever has /focus: not verified. Megaphone's TBC guide uses focus and
  arena1/2/3; Forever has no arenas per Teddys ("no plans right now to add any form of
  competitive PvP"). Design for mouseover.

### Mouse versus keyboard
- Consistent across every named source: the mouse gets instant, reactive, must-work-
  while-moving spells; the left hand keeps the cast-time rotation.
  - Xaryu: Fire Blast, Ice Block (+ Cold Snap on Shift), Polymorph on wheel.
  - Hansol: Counterspell, Scorch, Spellsteal, arena Poly/CS on the mouse; "it's two
    different sides of the brain".
  - Mark Gosse: Blink wheel up, Counterspell wheel down, Ice Barrier wheel click.
  - Megaphone: trinket + Nova on a mouse button.
- Nobody found puts Frostbolt on a thumb grid.

### ESDF versus WASD
- No named mage found on ESDF. Xaryu: movement keys not stated. Hansol: Q/W/E with A,S,D
  freed. Venruki recommends three movement keys only. Mark Gosse: A/D strafe.
- ESDF evidence is generic only: "I re-mapped WASD to ESDF for WoW to give me access to
  more keybinds. It has been a god-send... you also get the nice nipple on your index
  finger" (Jas09, Straight Dope, 2011, https://boards.straightdope.com/t/wasd-or-esdf/585546);
  two others in that thread want to but fear rebinding everything.
- The stronger convention is not which letters but: unbind backpedal and keyboard turn.
  Hansol, Venruki, Arwoht, Mark Gosse and Hazel all say it.

How they set it:
- Stick = forward + strafe, no backpedal, no turn. Hazel: "Make your forward W, your left
  E and your right Q. You can bind back to S if you really want... Congratulations, you
  just freed up three fingers." NieuBarmy: "set it to WSQE (so you strafe not rotate)".
  @4Syc321: "My stick is set to QWES... jump with my mouse 4 button... shift/control
  still used as modifiers."
- Jump has to move, because the default thumb paddle shares the thumb with the stick.
  Jag: jump on an index-finger key. @SlvrBckBlck: jump on a mouse button.
  @Xantosh82: "jumping while moving forward it is janky af on the tartarus".
- Use 4-way, not 8-way. NieuBarmy: "Make sure you only set up up down left and right and
  not the diagonals". Vulca123: "Set the joystick up for 4 way... The joystick now works
  in World of Warcraft properly." In 4-way mode the firmware presses both adjacent keys
  on a diagonal (grimZan, KrunkMonkey, polyretroVolt178 confirm after a replug/reboot).

### Known problems
- Diagonals. Synapse will not accept a two-key bind on the 8-way corners
  (https://insider.razer.com/razer-support-45/how-to-bind-all-8-directions-on-thumb-pad-5408,
  V2, 2019; Razer support's only answer was "create a macro"). Macros are "jerky (it
  makes my character look like he's having seizures)" (Arkandel) unless recorded with
  0.000s delays and "Play while assigned key is pressed" (coolBeaverflow516).
- Synapse regressions. June 2026: an update killed joystick input in WoW for a thread
  full of V2 and Pro owners (11 likes on the OP, at least 9 other posters reporting the same on page 1); two people bought
  a replacement unit before finding it was software. Fix was re-recording the stick binds.
- D-pad hardware wear: Arkandel (Orbweaver stick "chatter"), Appel-kun (V2 stick died
  after 4 years daily), WhoaUrTilted (W direction wore out), NieuBarmy ("about 5 now over
  the last 20 years"), @REVNAVISS (replacing V2 d-pad sensors), @omegabob99, @michaelmok3646,
  two mein-mmo commenters. About 9 independent reports.
- Sticking / ghost input on the V2 d-pad: OutbackIdaho, @Zeebo1uk, @stewynew ("NE, SE, SW
  and NW make the controller knob stick/catch"), @adrianconstantin1132 asks about it.
- The stick is digital, not analog, on the V2 and Pro (BigWynter; grettin for the G13).
- Fatigue / injury: mein-mmo reviewer with small hands got joint pain because "the hand,
  instead of relaxing, was tense the whole time" (German original,
  https://mein-mmo.de/en/razer-tartarus-v2-in-review-the-perfect-keypad-for-mmorpgs,219472/);
  @smittyjones8755 "nostromo/tartarus since day one. These will WRECK your wrist because
  of carpal tunnel. Have surgery on monday"; @Niiipon "my hands are just too small for
  it". Counter-reports of relief: @smarticle "no shoulder aches like I get from a
  keyboard", @PsilocybinRain, a mein-mmo commenter with small hands and no pain, NieuBarmy
  (arthritis / carpal tunnel "old boys").
- Learning curve: mein-mmo "extremely steep"; Jag "about a week"; a mein-mmo commenter
  "easily 2 weeks"; Scottejaye "weeks".
- V2 Pro only (released September 2026): thumb spacebar paddle and scroll wheel removed,
  stick reports as a right stick and is rotated 90 degrees. At least 10 commenters on the
  dazztrazak review call it a downgrade; 2 say they are returning it. Not relevant to a

## 4. Weak pinky / small hand ergonomics

- Direct match to the user: "my pinky finger is too weak and it hurts when I press both
  of those buttons [Shift and Alt]... I also have a 12 buttons mouse"
  (https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/keybinds-help/1272820, 2022-07, retail).
  Answers:
  - Modifiers on the mouse (xXTamagoXx): "I bind control and shift to very
    comfortable-to-press extra mouse buttons... My left hand presses no modifiers... never
    having to stretch my left hand further than 5... I spam M+ for hours on end and have
    never had any hand fatigue."
  - No modifiers at all (CutiePi): 12-button mouse plus Esc-F7, backtick, 1-6, Q W E R T,
    A S D F. "Figuring out that escape and `/~ could be used as binds was a huge game
    changer."
  - Ctrl with the palm (Shad); Shift with the ring finger (Masq); Alt with the thumb
    (CommandoCat, who can do Shift but not Alt/Ctrl with short fingers).
  - OP's conclusion: bind modifiers to the 12-button mouse.
- Limited-finger thread, Scimitar owner
  (https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/help-with-key-binds-for-left-hand-with-disability/839050,
  2021-01): "Trying to put my main rotation on the mouse has proven to be very
  uncomfortable. My thumb and hand fatigue very quickly and it's too hard to press them
  fast without accidentally pressing the wrong button." Advice given: modifiers on the
  mouse (Cokemanjr), a Tartarus/Orbweaver and float three fingers (Cokemanjr,
  Phantomblade), G600 third button as a Ctrl layer for 24 binds (EretTheLock), foot
  pedals, Caps Lock remapped to a modifier.
- Thumb as the modifier finger: Arwoht (Forever guide) "You press alt with your thumb and
  you can press Q and E easily"; Xaryu 2020 "I hit C with my thumb"; Venruki video title
  (thumb for Shift, Ctrl, Alt and Space).
- Key difficulty tiers from the Forever-specific guide (Arwoht, keyboard):
  best = tilde, 1-4, Q, E, R, F, C; good = 5, T, G, V, Z, X; only if you must = F1-F4, 6,
  Y, H, B. Free mouse binds: wheel up, wheel down, wheel click, mouse 4, mouse 5, each
  with Shift/Ctrl/Alt ("20 super easy to press keybinds").
- Caution on moving load to the thumb grid: Hansol (spamming with the thumb "feels kind
  of awkward"); @DrVombatus ("used an mmo mouse since cata but it's destroying my thumb
  years later"); @randywatson1982 (G600 "making my thumb hurt"); @fishontv123 ("mmo mouse
  hand pain"); Scottejaye ("I kept mis clicking on the naga with my thumb"); @semi6544
  ("I really only felt comfortable using 6 buttons" of 12); Tyrum above.
  5 independent thumb-strain or mis-press reports from 12-button mouse users, plus Hansol.
- A vendor blog on pinky fatigue (attackshark) surfaced in search; it uses modelled
  numbers, not measurements. Not used.
- No clinical or measured ergonomics source for MMO binds found. Everything here is
  self-report.

## 5. Keypad versus keyboard + 12-button MMO mouse

- What top mages use: keyboard + mouse, every one found. Xaryu a 2-side-button mouse,
  Hansol 5-button then G600 then Naga X, Mark Gosse a tilt-wheel mouse. No keypad user
  among named mages (not found).
- People running a keypad AND a 12-button mouse together: 14 found. Hazel (Orbweaver +
  G600: keypad 3x4 = rotation on F1-F12, mouse grid 1-12 = cooldowns, health pot,
  defensives; "Between my mouse and gamepad, I only touch my keyboard to type"),
  WhoaUrTilted (opposite split: basic rotation on the mouse grid, "more in-depth
  abilities" on the keypad), o0Rampancy0o, dazztrazak (V2 Pro + Naga; "I do enjoy a
  Scimitar as well"), Chiros, @stevencollins3501 ("39 buttons... 5 modifier functions"),
  @Shjandy, @TheAxiomChronos, @UnratedRetribution, @juanluna3901, @JoeyGarzaHealth, @qebm,
  @chookywookiee3924, @semi6544.
- Hazel's on-screen trick, repeated by several commenters: lay a 12-button bar out as the
  same 3x4 grid as the device so the screen matches the hand. Scottejaye stacks three
  12-button bars: plain, Shift, Alt.
- The split that matches the mage evidence (section 2): left hand = rotation and movement,
  thumb grid = low-frequency, reactive, must-not-miss buttons. Hazel's layout and Hansol's
  layout agree on this; WhoaUrTilted is the one dissent.
- Arguments for staying on the keypad, from sources: three fingers freed by thumb
  movement (Hazel, @morsmagne, mein-mmo), no hand drift ("no matter where I put my hand
  down... I immediately know where I'm at", WhoaUrTilted), comfort for some (BigWynter,
  @smarticle, NieuBarmy).
- Arguments for keyboard, from sources: thumb-movement retraining failed for some
  (@philbert9300, o0Rampancy0o, @Shjandy, @TheCrispyChip, BigWynter), d-pad wear and
  Synapse breakage (section 3), "you'll still need and use your keyboard" (BigWynter),
  small-hand reach (mein-mmo reviewer, @Niiipon), one blunt "gimmick" verdict
  (@Roundonelost).
- There is a middle path with evidence: keep the Tartarus but put movement on its keys and
  use the thumb pad for 2-4 binds (Approach B, 9 users). That keeps the fixed hand
  position and removes the two main Tartarus failure points (diagonals, thumb retraining)
  at the cost of three keys.
- No controlled comparison (APM, error rate) of keypad versus keyboard exists in anything
  I found. Hansol's "I swear it'd be much higher on the left hand" is a guess by him.
